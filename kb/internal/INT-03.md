# Handling angry and repeat callers (operator guidelines)

## Start with the history
Before answering, read the handover summary and the customer's recent cases. Never ask the customer to repeat what
the summary already says. Name the earlier contacts yourself: "I can see this is the third time the connection has
dropped since October."

## Acknowledge, then act
Acknowledge the frustration in one sentence, without excuses. Then say what you will do now and when the customer
will hear from us. Do not promise a fix time that no system has given you.

## Repeat faults
When the same fault comes back, do not book another standard visit without a new finding. Open an internal ticket
for the network team with the case history, and add a case comment. If the customer has 3 or more cases in the same
category within 60 days, check goodwill with the compensation tool (rule C3 needs a team lead's approval).

## Missed visits
If a booked technician did not arrive and the reason was on our side, apologise clearly and check the no-show
credit with the compensation tool (rule C2). Offer the earliest free window.
