# QN-Mesh Duo pairing and firmware recovery

## Satellite not paired
If the second unit shows a blinking amber light, it has lost the pairing. Ask the customer to place it within 3
metres of the main unit, press the pair button on both units within 2 minutes and wait for a steady white light.

## Firmware recovery
A unit that stays red after a restart needs firmware recovery. It is done remotely by provisioning from firmware
1.6.0 on; open an internal ticket. Older units are replaced by a technician visit.

## Wi-Fi channels
Mesh units choose channels automatically. A manual channel change on the main unit is kept for 24 hours, then the
automatic choice takes over again.
