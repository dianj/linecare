# Compensation and goodwill policy (internal)

## Principles
Credits are decided by staff, never promised by the customer assistant. Every credit is calculated by the
compensation tool, which implements the rules below; operators explain the result and do not calculate by hand.
Amounts are in EUR, rounded to 2 decimals.

## C1 Unplanned outage over 24 hours
For an unplanned internet or TV outage longer than 24 hours, the customer gets 1/30 of the monthly fee for each
started day of the outage, up to 50% of the monthly fee. An outage of exactly 24 hours does not qualify. Planned
maintenance announced at least 48 hours ahead gives no credit.

## C2 Technician no-show
If a booked technician does not arrive and the reason is on Quillnet's side, the customer gets a flat credit of
10 EUR.

## C3 Repeat fault goodwill
If the customer has 3 or more cases in the same category within 60 days, a goodwill credit of up to 15 EUR may be
given. This needs a team lead's approval: the tool returns "needs_approval".

## C4 Suspended accounts
No credit is given for any period in which the account was suspended for non-payment.
