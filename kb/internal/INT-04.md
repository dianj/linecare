# Outage communication procedure (internal)

## During an outage
Give customers the public cause and the expected fix time from the outage system, nothing more. Do not book
technicians and do not open individual cases for an outage that explains the customer's problem: the repair crew is
already working on it.

## When the expected time passes
If the expected fix time has passed and the outage is still active, tell the customer that the network team is
still working on it and that the time will be updated. Never invent a new time.

## After the outage
Unplanned outages longer than 24 hours may give a credit (rule C1 of the compensation policy). Customers do not have
to ask: the compensation tool calculates it per account when a customer contacts us about it.

## Planned maintenance
Planned work is announced at least 48 hours ahead by SMS and on the website. Announced planned work gives no credit.
