# Field visit checklist (technicians)

## Before the visit
Read the case, the line test history and the handover summary. Bring a spare router of the customer's model and a
line tester.

## At the customer
Test the line at the entry point first, then at the router. Replace damaged in-home cabling from the entry point to
the first socket at no cost; work beyond that is the customer's and costs a call-out fee of 25 EUR, agreed before
starting.

## Closing the visit
Add a case comment with the measured values and what was replaced, and set the appointment to done. If the visit
could not take place because of our side (wrong time, no technician), set it to "no_show" with the reason, so the
compensation tool can apply rule C2.
