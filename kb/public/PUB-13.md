# Privacy in support conversations

## What we keep
We keep the text of support conversations, the checks the assistant ran and their results, to solve your problem
and to improve our service. Your PIN and date of birth are never stored: they are removed from the conversation
before it is saved.

## Who can see it
Support staff see a summary of the conversation when you are handed over to a colleague, so you do not have to
repeat yourself. Staff never ask for your PIN.

## Voice conversations
In voice conversations, speech is converted to text on our own servers. The spoken replies are produced by an
external text-to-speech service, which receives the text of the reply only.

## How long
Conversation records are kept for 90 days, cases for as long as the law requires.
