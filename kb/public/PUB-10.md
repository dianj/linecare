## What "up to" means
The speed of a plan is the maximum of your connection at the router, measured with a cable. Over Wi-Fi the speed is
usually lower: walls, distance, older devices and neighbouring networks all reduce it. A speed test over Wi-Fi is
therefore not a measurement of your line.

## Measuring your speed correctly
Connect a computer to the router with a network cable, close other programs and devices that use the internet,
and run the test two or three times. If the cabled speed stays far below your plan, contact support so we can test
the line.

## Changing your plan
You can move to a faster plan at any time; the new fee applies from the next bill. Moving to a slower plan is
possible at the end of the contract period.
