# Technician visits

## When we send a technician
We book a visit when remote tests show a fault that cannot be fixed remotely: a line fault (no signal or a weak
signal with many errors), a router hardware fault, or repeated drops of the connection. We do not send a technician
during a known outage in your area (the repair crew is already working on it) or while an account is suspended.
Every visit is linked to a support case.

## Visit times
Technicians work Monday to Saturday, from 08:00 to 18:00, in two-hour windows (for example 10:00–12:00). The
support assistant offers the first free windows in your area and books the one you choose. You get an SMS
confirmation and a reminder the evening before.

## How to prepare
An adult (18+) must be at home for the whole window. Make sure the technician can reach the router and the point
where the cable enters your home. Keep pets away from the work area.

## Rescheduling and cancelling
You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to two hours before the window starts, through support chat or by
phone. Please tell us if the problem has gone away, so the slot can go to another customer.

## Cost
The visit is free when the fault is on Quillnet's side. If the technician finds that the cause is in your own
in-home wiring or devices, a call-out fee of 25 EUR may be charged; the technician tells you before starting any
paid work.
