# Moving house

## Tell us early
Tell us at least 14 days before your move, through support chat or by phone, with your new address and the date you
need the service there. We check whether the new address can get the same plan.

## Moving your service
If the new address is covered, moving the service is free and your contract continues unchanged. A technician
connects the new address; your router and set-top box move with you.

## If the new address is not covered
If your plan is not available at the new address, we offer the closest available plan. If none is available, you
can end the contract without an early termination fee, with proof of the new address.
