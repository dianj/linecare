# TV and set-top box: no signal or a frozen picture

## First check for an outage
If several channels stop at once, there may be a TV platform outage in your area. The support assistant can check
this for you; during an outage your internet keeps working and the picture comes back when the repair is done.

## No signal on the screen
Check that the TV is on the right input (HDMI 1 or HDMI 2, the one the set-top box is connected to) and that the
HDMI cable is pushed in firmly at both ends. The set-top box needs a working internet connection: if the router
lights show a problem, solve that first (see "Router lights explained").

## Frozen picture or a black screen
Restart the set-top box: unplug its power cable, wait 30 seconds and plug it back in. Starting up takes about two
minutes; do not unplug it again while the light is blinking. If only one channel is affected, try it again after
15 minutes before reporting it.

## Still not working
If the picture does not come back after a restart and there is no outage, contact support: we open a case and
check the box remotely.
