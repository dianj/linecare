# No internet: step-by-step checklist

## 1. Check for an outage first
Before changing anything at home, check whether there is a known outage in your area. Our support assistant can
check this for you, and affected customers also get an SMS. If there is an outage, nothing at home needs to be
changed: the connection comes back by itself when the repair is finished.

## 2. Look at the router lights
Check that the router is switched on and that the power light is steady. A red or blinking internet light means the
router has no connection to our network. See "Router lights explained" for what each light means on the QN-Box 3,
QN-Box 4 and QN-Mesh Duo.

## 3. Restart the router once
Many problems go away after one restart. Our assistant can restart the router remotely, or you can do it yourself:
unplug the power cable, wait 30 seconds and plug it back in. Wait about two minutes until the lights are steady.
Do not press the small reset hole: it erases your settings (see "Restarting your router safely").

## 4. Check the cables
Make sure the fibre or phone cable is firmly in the WAN or DSL port and is not bent sharply. If you use a wired
connection, try another network cable or another port on the router.

## 5. Only one device has no internet
If other devices work, the problem is most likely on that device. On the device: switch Wi-Fi off and on, "forget"
the Wi-Fi network and join it again with the password from the router label, and restart the device. Install
pending system updates. If the device still cannot connect but all other devices can, the fault is not on the
Quillnet line, and a technician visit will not help.

## 6. Still not working?
Contact support with your account number. We can run a remote line test and router check, and if we find a fault
on our side we open a case and, if needed, book a technician visit.
