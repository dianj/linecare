# Contract terms in short

## Contract period
Plans have a minimum contract period of 12 or 24 months (see "Plans and speeds"). After that period the contract
continues month by month.

## Cancelling
You can cancel with 30 days' notice. Within the minimum contract period an early termination fee applies: the
monthly fees for the remaining months, at most three monthly fees. After the minimum period there is no fee.

## Equipment
The router and the set-top box remain Quillnet's property. Return them within 30 days after the contract ends, in
any Quillnet shop or by free courier pickup; equipment that is not returned is charged at its current value.

## Credits for service problems
Credits for outages or missed technician visits are decided by our staff case by case, under our compensation
rules. The support assistant cannot promise or calculate a credit; it can open a case or connect you with a
colleague.
