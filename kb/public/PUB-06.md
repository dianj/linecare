# Planned maintenance and outages

## How we inform you
Planned maintenance is announced at least 48 hours ahead by SMS and on the Quillnet status page. For unplanned
outages (for example a cable damaged during road works), affected customers get an SMS as soon as the fault is
confirmed, and again when it is fixed.

## The expected fix time
For every outage we publish an expected fix time. It is an estimate from the repair crew and may change; we update
it on the status page. You do not need to report an outage that is already known, and you do not need a technician
visit: the service comes back without any action at home.

## What to do during an outage
Do not reset the router or change its settings: this does not help and can cause problems after the repair. If
your service is still not working two hours after the outage was fixed, restart the router once and contact support.

## Credits for long outages
Long unplanned outages may qualify for a credit on your bill under our terms. Credits are checked and applied by
our staff; the support assistant cannot promise or calculate a credit.
