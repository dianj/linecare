# Payment methods

## Card payment online
Pay by debit or credit card in the Quillnet app or on the customer web portal. The payment is registered at once.
Support staff and the support assistant never ask for your card details in chat or by phone.

## Direct debit
Set up a monthly direct debit from your bank account in the customer portal. The amount is collected on the due
date, so bills are never late.

## Bank transfer
Transfer the amount to the bank account printed on your bill and use your account number (for example QN-100042)
as the payment reference. Transfers can take up to one working day to arrive.

## Cash
Pay in cash at any Quillnet shop or at partner payment points. Bring your account number. Cash payments are
registered within an hour.
