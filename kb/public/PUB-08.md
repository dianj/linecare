# Bills, late payment and suspension

## Your monthly bill
Bills are issued on the 1st of each month for the current month and are due on the 20th. Your bill shows your
plan, any extra charges, the amount due and the account number to use when paying.

## Late payment
If a bill is not paid by the due date, we send a reminder by SMS and email. There is no late-payment fee for the
first reminder.

## Suspension
If a bill is still unpaid 14 days after its due date, the services on the account are suspended: internet and TV
stop working, but you can still reach support. A suspended account shows no technical fault, and a technician
visit or router restart will not restore the service.

## Reactivation
Pay the overdue amount with any payment method (see "Payment methods"). The service is reactivated automatically,
usually within two hours after the payment is registered; bank transfers can take up to one working day to arrive.
There is no reactivation fee. If you cannot pay the full amount at once, our staff can discuss a payment plan with
you.
