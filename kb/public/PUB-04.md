# Restarting your router safely

## Restart, not reset
A restart switches the router off and on and keeps all your settings. A factory reset (pressing the small reset
hole for 10 seconds) erases your Wi-Fi name, password and the connection settings, and the router may not reconnect
to our network without help. Do not reset the router unless support asks you to.

## Remote restart by support
Our support assistant can restart your router remotely after you agree. Internet, Wi-Fi and TV are down for about
two minutes during the restart. For safety, support makes at most two remote restarts in one conversation.

## Restarting it yourself
Unplug the router's power cable, wait 30 seconds, plug it back in, and wait about two minutes until the lights are
steady. Then check the connection on one device first.

## When a restart does not help
If the problem comes back after a restart, contact support again: we will check the line and the router remotely
and open a case if a fault is found.
